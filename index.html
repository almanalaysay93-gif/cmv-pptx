CMV Biobank, Davao Region

Host clinical factors and CMV strains among kidney transplant patients, multicenter hospitals in Davao Region

Background

CMV threatens kidney transplant recipients

Immunosuppressants prevent rejection but weaken control of viral infection.

CMV can reactivate or cause primary infection, raising the risk of graft rejection, other infections, organ dysfunction, morbidity and mortality.

The barrier

Prophylaxis works, but cost is a barrier

Valganciclovir is proven for CMV prophylaxis and treatment.

It is given from 2 to 6 months post-transplant, yet its high cost burdens patients on immunosuppressive therapy.

Study population

40

kidney transplant donor-recipient pairs

Followed through six timepoints: pre-transplantation, post-immunosuppression and post-prophylaxis.

A CMV biobank supports research on biomarkers, drug testing, diagnostic validation and longitudinal CMV dynamics.

Objectives

Main objective

To establish a biobank for the characterization of CMV strains and the clinical host status of kidney transplant patients in Region XI.

Activities

Key project activities

  1. Patient orientation, recruitment and informed consent
  2. Specimen collection and processing
  3. CMV PCR quantitative analysis across all timepoints
  4. Seroprevalence monitoring
  5. Strain determination for all positive patients
  6. Classifying donor-derived, primary or reactivation
  7. Data analysis and interpretation

Recruitment

Patient recruitment and blood extraction

Recruitment happens during pre-kidney transplant orientation lectures, followed by blood extraction for CMV antigenemia and the CMV antibody panel.

Laboratory

CMV QNAT, extraction and transport

Quantitative nucleic acid testing for CMV, DNA extraction at PGC Mindanao, and transport of samples for sequencing.

Sequencing and data

Raw CMV sequences and the biobank database

Bioinformatics pipelines and Python scripts collect, store and analyze the data. The SUBAY CMV biobank database app records each sample.

Training

Training on CMV PCR and the biobank

Staff trained on the COBAS 5800 CMV PCR system and on biobank handling and storage.

Challenges and outputs

Catch-up plan and expected outputs

Delays in procurement

The ultralow freezer, refrigerator and liquid nitrogen tank failed the bidding process. Suppliers did not submit the CMDR required by FDA.

Catch-up: request SPMC counterpart funding for the ultralow freezer.

Slow patient recruitment

Recruitment depends on the timing of kidney transplantation. Clearance and ethics approval vary per patient.

Catch-up: sample collection schedule plotted to reach the required sample size.
